# Unit 65b: research on design research and brainstorming (web, 2026-11-27)

The research behind 65b's interview: how other tools research a design question and brainstorm it with a person, and the risks of web research by an agent. Gathered by a research run in the interview session; every claim has its source.


- GitHub Spec Kit: /clarify ≤5 questions, one at a time, "Recommended: Option X"; answers logged in the spec under "## Clarifications / ### Session YYYY-MM-DD / - Q: … → A: …". /plan Phase 0: research agents per unknown → research.md entries "Decision / Rationale / Alternatives considered"; no web-search or citation rules. https://github.com/github/spec-kit/blob/main/templates/commands/plan.md , .../clarify.md
- BMAD: brainstorming (human picks the AI's role); deep-recon researches a DECISION, the human approves the question plan first, every claim sourced and dated, comparisons end with pick + runner-up + strongest counter-argument; the architect agent lays out options and leans, the human decides; it records only decisions two builders could make incompatibly, each with a stable ID. https://docs.bmad-method.org/clarify/clarify-the-idea/ , https://docs.bmad-method.org/plan/design-ux-and-architecture/
- Kiro: requirements.md (EARS) → design.md → tasks.md; comments on passages; web tools disabled client-side only; no documented research step in spec mode. https://kiro.dev/docs/specs/ , https://kiro.dev/docs/enterprise/governance/web-tools/
- Claude Code: subagents with a tools: allowlist; WebFetch(domain:) rules; fetches summarized by a separate model; API web search has allowed_domains, max_uses, cited_text. Anthropic multi-agent research: parallel workers, a separate CitationAgent, source-quality prompts (SEO farms). https://code.claude.com/docs/en/permissions , https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/agents-and-tools/tool-use/web-search-tool , https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/built-multi-agent-research-system
- Deep Research (OpenAI, Gemini, Perplexity): inline citations; sources consulted vs cited kept apart; Gemini shows an editable research plan before it starts; domain filters are leaky (Perplexity). https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/deep-research , https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15719111
- MADR: Considered Options, Decision Drivers, More Information (links). https://adr.github.io/madr/
- Risks: the lethal trifecta (private data + untrusted content + outbound channel: cut one leg) https://simonwillison.net/2025/Jun/16/the-lethal-trifecta/ ; citation hallucination >60% wrong in Tow Center tests https://www.cjr.org/tow_center/we-compared-eight-ai-search-engines-theyre-all-bad-at-citing-news.php ; enforce at the sandbox/network layer, not in the prompt.
