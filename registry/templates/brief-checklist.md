# Brief checklist

For the verifier. Each line is a yes / no question about the brief as written. The lint has
already checked the shape, the IDs and the writing rules; these need judgement.

## The summary for approval

- Could the approver decide from the summary alone, without opening the detail?
- Do the summary and the detail say the same thing: no claim, number or promise in the summary
  that the detail doesn't hold, and nothing important in the detail that the summary hides?
- Is every real choice under "Decisions for you", with options and a recommendation, rather than
  decided quietly in the detail?
- Does "The ask" state the outcome, not the activity ("cut drop-off to 25%", not "work on checkout")?

## The detail

- Is the problem a real one, with evidence, and is every assumption marked as one?
- Is the audience one real user or role in a real situation, not "everyone"?
- Does each success metric measure the outcome, not the output, and is its counter-metric the
  thing most likely to get worse?
- Is each business requirement an outcome, testable later through the PRD's requirements, and
  free of any solution (a screen, a field, an API)?
- Are the no-gos the things a reader would otherwise expect, so the agents don't build them?
- Are the rabbit holes the real risks, each with a way through or a no-go?
- Is the appetite a limit worth keeping, and does the track fit the work's size and risk?
- Would an agent writing the PRD from this brief have to guess anything? Name it.
- Is anything left from the template: a guidance line, a placeholder, the sample id?
