# Business change checklist

For the person who approves it. The lint has already checked the shape: each change has its
"Replace:" and "With:" quotes, a source and a reason, and MIRAI checks at approval that each
"Replace:" text is in business.md exactly once.

## Each change

- Does the new text say what you said, and only that?
- Does every new claim end with its source ID, with a row for a new source under "Sources"?
- Would the rest of business.md still hold with this change: no other line now contradicts it?
- Is anything left from the template: a guidance line, a sample line, the sample id?
